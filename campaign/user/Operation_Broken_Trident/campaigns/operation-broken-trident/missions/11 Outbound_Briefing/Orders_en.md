# 11 Outbound

*Names rather than tonnage*

## COMMANDER'S DISPATCH

Meridian Star's manifest is now a list of people. Keep the transport moving toward the assigned safety area, even if a more satisfying battle offers itself elsewhere.

## SITUATION

M/V Meridian Star is carrying evacuees through an eastbound safety leg in the Ionian Sea. 'Outbound' describes departure from danger, not a westbound route: follow the marked area and the transport's actual track. Cabin lights and ordinary merchant routines continue around a movement whose passengers understand very little of the tactical picture. Their dependence on the escort is complete.

## MISSION / COMPLETION CONDITIONS

Keep M/V Meridian Star afloat and bring her into the marked safety area within 180 minutes. Preserve at least one task-group vessel.

## THREAT ASSESSMENT

A Kilo, surface attackers and mixed air opposition are assessed. Civilian traffic may share bearings with hostile units, while a fishing boat's intermittent return can resemble an unresolved small combatant. The transport's identity must remain clear throughout the engagement.

## EXECUTION

Stay with Meridian Star until she reaches the safety area. Resist being pulled away by surface contacts while maintaining an underwater watch. This sortie offers no pre-mission repair or rearm; allocate the force you actually retain. Saving ammunition for the finale is worthwhile only if the evacuation ship survives this leg.

## RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

Positive identification is required before attacking surface contacts. Hostile aircraft, missiles and positively identified enemy combatants may be engaged in defence of the task. Radar silence, nationality or proximity to a warship alone does not establish hostile intent. Protect the assigned transport where present; do not attack neutral traffic.

## AVIATION / SUSTAINMENT

No pre-mission rearm or repair is offered for this sortie. Use Souda Bay Air Base (Crete) for aircraft you have purchased, assigned and prepared. No free friendly combat aircraft are promised. Ships and ammunition state carry through the campaign.

## CONDITIONS / NOTES

29 September 1985, 05:55Z. Sea state 3; overcast cloud; wind from NW. This is a fictional 1985 crisis. Doctrine guidance is advisory; completion conditions are stated explicitly above.
