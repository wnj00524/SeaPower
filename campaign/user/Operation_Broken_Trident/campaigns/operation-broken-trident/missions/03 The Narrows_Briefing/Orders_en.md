# 03 The Narrows

*Cargo before prestige*

## COMMANDER'S DISPATCH

The Harbor Crown is neither fast nor expendable. You have been sent to deliver a ship, not to collect a list of enemy hulls sunk.

## SITUATION

Sicilian radio stations are reading casualty rumours alongside ordinary weather reports. M/V Harbor Crown is carrying urgent logistics through the southeastern approaches to Sicily. The open-water lane offers room to manoeuvre, but the traffic around it offers cover to a missile craft looking for a short engagement. A successful delivery will keep the supply timetable credible; a detached escort chasing an attractive target could undo it in minutes.

## MISSION / COMPLETION CONDITIONS

Keep M/V Harbor Crown afloat and bring her into the marked safety area within 180 minutes. Preserve at least one task-group vessel.

## THREAT ASSESSMENT

Missile craft and a frigate screen are assessed in the area. Fighters and a small bomber element may operate beyond visual range. A radar-silent return is ambiguous: it may be a fishing boat, a cautious merchant or an attacker using another unit's picture.

## EXECUTION

Identify Harbor Crown and the exit area before accelerating the task group. Keep your defence arranged around her progress rather than the loudest emitter. Maintain positive identification of surface targets and consider the merchant traffic beyond the intended missile target.

## RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

Positive identification is required before attacking surface contacts. Hostile aircraft, missiles and positively identified enemy combatants may be engaged in defence of the task. Radar silence, nationality or proximity to a warship alone does not establish hostile intent. Protect the assigned transport where present; do not attack neutral traffic.

## AVIATION / SUSTAINMENT

Rearm and repair are offered before this mission. Use NAS Sigonella (Sicily) for aircraft you have purchased, assigned and prepared. No free friendly combat aircraft are promised. Ships and ammunition state carry through the campaign.

## CONDITIONS / NOTES

11 September 1985, 11:45Z. Sea state 2; scattered cloud; wind from NW. This is a fictional 1985 crisis. Doctrine guidance is advisory; completion conditions are stated explicitly above.
