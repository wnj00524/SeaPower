# 04 Crete Barrier

*The barrier has a gap*

## COMMANDER'S DISPATCH

Souda can prepare your aircraft. It cannot make the water transparent. Close the submarine threat before the next movement enters the approaches.

## SITUATION

The campaign's weight is shifting east. South of Crete, a Soviet Kilo threatens the space between shore-based aviation and the fleet's working routes. Its silence is the problem: there may be no dramatic warning until an escort is already reacting to a torpedo. The surface screen and aircraft activity are conspicuous enough to split a commander's attention at exactly the wrong time.

## MISSION / COMPLETION CONDITIONS

Destroy the designated Kilo submarine within 180 minutes. Preserve at least one task-group vessel. Additional escorts and shore sites are not compulsory targets.

## THREAT ASSESSMENT

A Kilo-class diesel-electric boat is the designated target. Frigate and destroyer support is assessed, with fighter patrols and Tu-16 bombers possible. Exact submarine position is withheld because it is not established by the briefing intelligence. Shore radars and SAM sites defend expeditionary aviation approaches farther south.

## EXECUTION

Conduct the hunt without allowing the group to become a succession of isolated sonar platforms. Use prepared aviation if available, preserve an air-defence watch and distinguish a firm underwater solution from a convenient bearing. Do not assume a quiet sea is an empty one.

## RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

Positive identification is required before attacking surface contacts. Hostile aircraft, missiles and positively identified enemy combatants may be engaged in defence of the task. Radar silence, nationality or proximity to a warship alone does not establish hostile intent. Protect the assigned transport where present; do not attack neutral traffic.

## AVIATION / SUSTAINMENT

No pre-mission rearm or repair is offered for this sortie. Use Souda Bay Air Base (Crete) for aircraft you have purchased, assigned and prepared. No free friendly combat aircraft are promised. Ships and ammunition state carry through the campaign.

## CONDITIONS / NOTES

13 September 1985, 02:20Z. Sea state 3; broken cloud; wind from NW. This is a fictional 1985 crisis. Doctrine guidance is advisory; completion conditions are stated explicitly above.
