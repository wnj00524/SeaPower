# 05 The Black Sea Squadron

*Unpick the screen*

## COMMANDER'S DISPATCH

The opposing detachment does not need to win a fleet action. It needs to keep your sensors and weapons occupied while the next raid develops.

## SITUATION

Southeast of Crete, Soviet surface units are gathering under a wider air threat. The ships in front of you are a screen, not the whole enemy effort. Command wants its designated Kashin and Krivak pair removed before that screen can continue shaping the approach. Additional escorts may intervene, but an expanding battle must not obscure the two ships that define your task.

## MISSION / COMPLETION CONDITIONS

Destroy the designated Kashin and Krivak within 150 minutes. Preserve at least one task-group vessel. Additional escorts and shore sites are not compulsory targets.

## THREAT ASSESSMENT

Destroyer and frigate opposition is assessed alongside Tu-16 and Tu-22M2 strike aircraft with fighter cover. Intercepts suggest activity from more than one bearing; they do not establish a timed or synchronized attack. Emitting screens may draw attention away from a silent missile platform.

## EXECUTION

Locate and identify the designated Kashin and Krivak targets. Arrange reconnaissance, air defence and surface attack as parts of one plan. A large surface salvo followed by an empty defensive magazine would leave the surviving group vulnerable to the aircraft still outside its picture.

## RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

Positive identification is required before attacking surface contacts. Hostile aircraft, missiles and positively identified enemy combatants may be engaged in defence of the task. Radar silence, nationality or proximity to a warship alone does not establish hostile intent. Protect the assigned transport where present; do not attack neutral traffic.

## AVIATION / SUSTAINMENT

Rearm and repair are offered before this mission. Use Souda Bay Air Base (Crete) for aircraft you have purchased, assigned and prepared. No free friendly combat aircraft are promised. Ships and ammunition state carry through the campaign.

## CONDITIONS / NOTES

15 September 1985, 13:30Z. Sea state 3; broken cloud; wind from NW. This is a fictional 1985 crisis. Doctrine guidance is advisory; completion conditions are stated explicitly above.
