# 02 Broken Contact

*A noise beneath the traffic*

## COMMANDER'S DISPATCH

A contact report is not a position. Treat the last bearing as the beginning of the search, and keep your attention on the water while the enemy tries to draw it into the sky.

## SITUATION

The Ionian approaches are crowded with ships changing routes after the first warnings. A Soviet Foxtrot has been reported near the convoy approach, but the latest acoustic trace broke among merchant machinery noise. A surfaced escort is easier to see and easier to hate; it is not the assigned quarry. The submarine's presence alone can close a route that NATO cannot afford to abandon.

## MISSION / COMPLETION CONDITIONS

Destroy the designated Foxtrot submarine within 180 minutes. Preserve at least one task-group vessel. Additional escorts and shore sites are not compulsory targets.

## THREAT ASSESSMENT

A diesel-electric submarine is the designated threat. Surface escorts, fighters and Tu-16 activity complicate the hunt. The submarine's exact location and depth are unconfirmed. Merchant noise may mask it, while an overly aggressive search can expose your ships to the surface and air screen.

## EXECUTION

Build a stable contact before attacking. If you purchased and prepared maritime patrol aircraft, use them to extend the search while surface ships retain mutual air defence. Avoid spending the entire ammunition reserve on visible escorts when the submarine remains unlocated.

## RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

Positive identification is required before attacking surface contacts. Hostile aircraft, missiles and positively identified enemy combatants may be engaged in defence of the task. Radar silence, nationality or proximity to a warship alone does not establish hostile intent. Protect the assigned transport where present; do not attack neutral traffic.

## AVIATION / SUSTAINMENT

No pre-mission rearm or repair is offered for this sortie. Use NAS Sigonella (Sicily) for aircraft you have purchased, assigned and prepared. No free friendly combat aircraft are promised. Ships and ammunition state carry through the campaign.

## CONDITIONS / NOTES

09 September 1985, 21:15Z. Sea state 3; overcast cloud; wind from NW. This is a fictional 1985 crisis. Doctrine guidance is advisory; completion conditions are stated explicitly above.
