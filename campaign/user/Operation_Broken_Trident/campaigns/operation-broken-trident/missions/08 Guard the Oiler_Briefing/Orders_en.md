# 08 Guard the Oiler

*The ship that keeps ships moving*

## COMMANDER'S DISPATCH

An oiler makes a poor headline and an excellent target. Stonehaven is the reason another task group will have choices tomorrow.

## SITUATION

USNS Stonehaven is approaching a replenishment rendezvous in the central Mediterranean. Every combatant wants fuel, but the immediate task is simpler than an actual replenishment evolution: keep the oiler alive and escort her into the marked safety area. Enemy surface units can threaten the support ship while bombers force your defensive attention upward. The ordinary freighters around the rendezvous make rapid visual assumptions particularly dangerous.

## MISSION / COMPLETION CONDITIONS

Keep USNS Stonehaven (fictional) afloat and bring her into the marked safety area within 180 minutes. Preserve at least one task-group vessel.

## THREAT ASSESSMENT

Missile craft and frigate/destroyer escorts are assessed, with mixed bomber and fighter activity. Enemy facilities on the Libyan coast support the wider air threat. Stonehaven is a protected support ship represented on the neutral side; this does not make her an expendable background contact.

## EXECUTION

Find the oiler first, then build a protective screen around her route. Keep sufficient air-defence capacity for contacts not yet visible. Break off a pursuit if it opens a direct approach to Stonehaven. No underway-transfer mechanic is required to complete this mission.

## RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

Positive identification is required before attacking surface contacts. Hostile aircraft, missiles and positively identified enemy combatants may be engaged in defence of the task. Radar silence, nationality or proximity to a warship alone does not establish hostile intent. Protect the assigned transport where present; do not attack neutral traffic.

## AVIATION / SUSTAINMENT

Rearm and repair are offered before this mission. Use Souda Bay Air Base (Crete) for aircraft you have purchased, assigned and prepared. No free friendly combat aircraft are promised. Ships and ammunition state carry through the campaign.

## CONDITIONS / NOTES

22 September 1985, 18:10Z. Sea state 3; broken cloud; wind from NW. This is a fictional 1985 crisis. Doctrine guidance is advisory; completion conditions are stated explicitly above.
