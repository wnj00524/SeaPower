# 09 Sunken Spear

*The command ship and its shield*

## COMMANDER'S DISPATCH

The cruiser matters. So does the escort that keeps it useful. Your orders name both; sinking the largest hull alone will not finish this task.

## SITUATION

A Slava-class missile cruiser is assessed to be directing the forward surface effort in the eastern Mediterranean. It carries enough striking power to demand attention on its own, but command has identified the cruiser and its designated Krivak escort as a pair that must be removed. Other escorts and a Kilo complicate the approach. With no rearm stop for this sortie, the attack must leave the force capable of defending itself.

## MISSION / COMPLETION CONDITIONS

Destroy the Slava cruiser and designated Krivak escort within 150 minutes. Preserve at least one task-group vessel. Additional escorts and shore sites are not compulsory targets.

## THREAT ASSESSMENT

Expect layered surface defence, submarine risk and mixed bomber flights with fighters. Exact ship positions are not established by the chart. A prominent emitter may be a screen rather than the cruiser. Reports of shore radar coverage do not justify treating every offshore contact as a member of that network.

## EXECUTION

Confirm the Slava and the designated Krivak before committing attack weapons. Retain a defence against the aircraft that may arrive during or after the surface exchange. Do not let a damaged primary target draw ships out of mutual support into the submarine threat.

## RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

Positive identification is required before attacking surface contacts. Hostile aircraft, missiles and positively identified enemy combatants may be engaged in defence of the task. Radar silence, nationality or proximity to a warship alone does not establish hostile intent. Protect the assigned transport where present; do not attack neutral traffic.

## AVIATION / SUSTAINMENT

No pre-mission rearm or repair is offered for this sortie. Use Souda Bay Air Base (Crete) for aircraft you have purchased, assigned and prepared. No free friendly combat aircraft are promised. Ships and ammunition state carry through the campaign.

## CONDITIONS / NOTES

25 September 1985, 22:35Z. Sea state 4; scattered cloud; wind from NW. This is a fictional 1985 crisis. Doctrine guidance is advisory; completion conditions are stated explicitly above.
