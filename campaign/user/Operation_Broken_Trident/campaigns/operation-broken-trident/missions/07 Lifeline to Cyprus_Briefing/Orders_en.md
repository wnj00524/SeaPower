# 07 Lifeline to Cyprus

*A promise in the manifest*

## COMMANDER'S DISPATCH

The Levant Promise's cargo has a destination and a list of people waiting for it. Keep those facts in view when the radar begins to fill.

## SITUATION

The relief freighter M/V Levant Promise is making an eastbound tactical leg toward the Cyprus approaches. Her arrival in the marked safety area will complete your part of the movement; this scenario does not depict unloading at a Cypriot port. Syrian access facilities now support a fictional Soviet expeditionary detachment. That broadens the air picture while civilian shipping continues through waters that neither side can make exclusively military.

## MISSION / COMPLETION CONDITIONS

Keep M/V Levant Promise afloat and bring her into the marked safety area within 180 minutes. Preserve at least one task-group vessel.

## THREAT ASSESSMENT

Surface combatants, a diesel-electric submarine, mixed bomber flights and fighter cover are assessed. Enemy shore defences protect the Syrian staging area, not an impenetrable blanket over the whole sea. Merchant and fishing contacts remain neutral unless independently identified as hostile.

## EXECUTION

Protect the freighter's lane while preserving both air and underwater awareness. Purchased aircraft may use RAF Akrotiri under the campaign's fictional access arrangement, provided they were prepared. Avoid chasing an emitter toward defended shore approaches while the relief ship remains exposed offshore.

## RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

Positive identification is required before attacking surface contacts. Hostile aircraft, missiles and positively identified enemy combatants may be engaged in defence of the task. Radar silence, nationality or proximity to a warship alone does not establish hostile intent. Protect the assigned transport where present; do not attack neutral traffic.

## AVIATION / SUSTAINMENT

Rearm and repair are offered before this mission. Use RAF Akrotiri (Cyprus) for aircraft you have purchased, assigned and prepared. No free friendly combat aircraft are promised. Ships and ammunition state carry through the campaign.

## CONDITIONS / NOTES

20 September 1985, 06:00Z. Sea state 2; scattered cloud; wind from NW. This is a fictional 1985 crisis. Doctrine guidance is advisory; completion conditions are stated explicitly above.
