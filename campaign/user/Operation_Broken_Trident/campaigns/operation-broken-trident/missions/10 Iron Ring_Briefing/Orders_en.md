# 10 Iron Ring

*Three links in the ring*

## COMMANDER'S DISPATCH

Break the designated screen, not your own formation. Command needs three confirmed losses on the other side and ships left on ours.

## SITUATION

The Soviet screen between Crete and the eastern approaches has become a network of mutually supporting capabilities. Your assigned targets are an Udaloy, a Kara and a Krivak. None is the campaign's final command ship; all three matter to the screen's ability to detect, protect and hold water. A fourth escort and air opposition can still turn a technically successful attack into a costly preparation for the finale.

## MISSION / COMPLETION CONDITIONS

Destroy the designated Udaloy, Kara and Krivak within 150 minutes. Preserve at least one task-group vessel. Additional escorts and shore sites are not compulsory targets.

## THREAT ASSESSMENT

The designated three-ship screen is assessed with an additional destroyer and mixed bomber/fighter activity. Weapons and sensors overlap unevenly; a radar-silent ship is not necessarily disconnected from the group. No exact enemy positions are supplied on the briefing chart.

## EXECUTION

Identify the Udaloy, Kara and assigned Krivak, then plan how to defeat the whole target set within the window. Separate target priorities from automatic responses to the nearest contact. Preserve enough sensors, air defence and ammunition to bring the force out after the third required target is destroyed.

## RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

Positive identification is required before attacking surface contacts. Hostile aircraft, missiles and positively identified enemy combatants may be engaged in defence of the task. Radar silence, nationality or proximity to a warship alone does not establish hostile intent. Protect the assigned transport where present; do not attack neutral traffic.

## AVIATION / SUSTAINMENT

Rearm and repair are offered before this mission. Use Souda Bay Air Base (Crete) for aircraft you have purchased, assigned and prepared. No free friendly combat aircraft are promised. Ships and ammunition state carry through the campaign.

## CONDITIONS / NOTES

27 September 1985, 07:50Z. Sea state 3; broken cloud; wind from NW. This is a fictional 1985 crisis. Doctrine guidance is advisory; completion conditions are stated explicitly above.
