# 12 Broken Trident

*The last operational order*

## COMMANDER'S DISPATCH

There is talk of a ceasefire. There is also a strike group still at sea. Until an order changes your task, the ships in front of you remain the problem you must solve.

## SITUATION

South of Crete, the last major Soviet surface strike group in this campaign is still operational. Diplomatic messages offer a possible end to the crisis without giving either tactical command a reliable timetable. Your designated targets are the Slava command cruiser, its Kara escort and the assigned Krivak. Additional escorts, a Kilo and the strongest air opposition of the campaign can still impose losses after the first successful hit.

## MISSION / COMPLETION CONDITIONS

Destroy the Slava, Kara and designated Krivak within 150 minutes. Preserve at least one task-group vessel. Additional escorts and shore sites are not compulsory targets.

## THREAT ASSESSMENT

Expect mixed Tu-16 and Tu-22M2 strike aircraft, fighter cover, submarine risk and layered surface defence. Reporting remains incomplete. A pause in emissions is not a ceasefire, and an unidentified merchant remains a civilian contact until evidence establishes otherwise.

## EXECUTION

Use the ships, aircraft and magazines that survived the campaign. Confirm the three assigned targets and keep the force mutually supporting through the attack. Do not pursue optional shore targets at the expense of the required ships. This operation has no repair or rearm opportunity before launch.

## RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

Positive identification is required before attacking surface contacts. Hostile aircraft, missiles and positively identified enemy combatants may be engaged in defence of the task. Radar silence, nationality or proximity to a warship alone does not establish hostile intent. Protect the assigned transport where present; do not attack neutral traffic.

## AVIATION / SUSTAINMENT

No pre-mission rearm or repair is offered for this sortie. Use Souda Bay Air Base (Crete) for aircraft you have purchased, assigned and prepared. No free friendly combat aircraft are promised. Ships and ammunition state carry through the campaign.

## CONDITIONS / NOTES

01 October 1985, 16:20Z. Sea state 4; broken cloud; wind from NW. This is a fictional 1985 crisis. Doctrine guidance is advisory; completion conditions are stated explicitly above.
