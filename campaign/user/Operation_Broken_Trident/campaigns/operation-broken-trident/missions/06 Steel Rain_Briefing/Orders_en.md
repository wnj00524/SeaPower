# 06 Steel Rain

*Seventy-five minutes*

## COMMANDER'S DISPATCH

Keep the group alive for seventy-five minutes. Do not turn a survival order into a pursuit order because the first attacker offers you a target.

## SITUATION

The picket south of Crete is now a place the opposing command expects NATO to occupy. That makes it useful and dangerous. The first aircraft you detect may be a fighter clearing a route, a missile carrier or an attempt to force an early expenditure of ammunition. Command has defined a finite holding window. Your duty is to bring surviving ships through that window with a coherent defence.

## MISSION / COMPLETION CONDITIONS

Keep at least one task-group vessel alive until 75 minutes have elapsed. No geographic station-keeping or enemy-kill condition is scripted.

## THREAT ASSESSMENT

Mixed Tu-16 and Tu-22M2 strike aircraft, fighter cover and a surface group including a Kara are assessed. Different approach ranges may separate their arrival times. Do not rely on all aircraft appearing in a single wave, or on an enemy radar remaining active throughout an engagement.

## EXECUTION

Establish identification and defensive priorities early. Use your prepared fighters, if available, to disrupt launch platforms before shipboard missiles become the only defence. Keep the group mutually supporting and watch the remaining magazine after each engagement. Pursuit is discretionary; survival is the objective.

## RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

Positive identification is required before attacking surface contacts. Hostile aircraft, missiles and positively identified enemy combatants may be engaged in defence of the task. Radar silence, nationality or proximity to a warship alone does not establish hostile intent. Protect the assigned transport where present; do not attack neutral traffic.

## AVIATION / SUSTAINMENT

No pre-mission rearm or repair is offered for this sortie. Use Souda Bay Air Base (Crete) for aircraft you have purchased, assigned and prepared. No free friendly combat aircraft are promised. Ships and ammunition state carry through the campaign.

## CONDITIONS / NOTES

17 September 1985, 04:45Z. Sea state 4; scattered cloud; wind from NW. This is a fictional 1985 crisis. Doctrine guidance is advisory; completion conditions are stated explicitly above.
