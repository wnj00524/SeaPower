# 01 Sicilian Passage

*The first ship through*

## COMMANDER'S DISPATCH

The Sea Amber's master has asked whether he should turn back. Sixth Fleet has told him his escort is coming. That assurance now rests with you.

## SITUATION

A patrol aircraft has been lost south of Crete and both commands are transmitting accusations faster than verified reports. Commercial masters have received contradictory warnings. M/V Sea Amber is carrying emergency stores for Malta through a southern safety lane; her crew cannot wait for the diplomatic picture to clear. Soviet small combatants are moving among ordinary shipping. Their intentions may be hostile, but a merchant hull seen near a warship is still a merchant hull.

## MISSION / COMPLETION CONDITIONS

Keep M/V Sea Amber afloat and bring her into the marked safety area within 180 minutes. Preserve at least one task-group vessel.

## THREAT ASSESSMENT

Reports suggest missile craft with frigate support and fighter activity. Confidence is moderate on force types, low on individual positions. Some attackers may remain radar-silent while an escort emits. There is no confirmed bomber raid in this opening operation.

## EXECUTION

Keep a screen between the transport and developing contacts without leaving her exposed to a different bearing. Use reconnaissance to identify small returns before committing missiles. The assigned safety area is south of Malta; do not cut across the island while pursuing a contact.

## RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

Positive identification is required before attacking surface contacts. Hostile aircraft, missiles and positively identified enemy combatants may be engaged in defence of the task. Radar silence, nationality or proximity to a warship alone does not establish hostile intent. Protect the assigned transport where present; do not attack neutral traffic.

## AVIATION / SUSTAINMENT

Rearm and repair are offered before this mission. Use NAS Sigonella (Sicily) for aircraft you have purchased, assigned and prepared. No free friendly combat aircraft are promised. Ships and ammunition state carry through the campaign.

## CONDITIONS / NOTES

08 September 1985, 05:30Z. Sea state 2; scattered cloud; wind from NW. This is a fictional 1985 crisis. Doctrine guidance is advisory; completion conditions are stated explicitly above.
