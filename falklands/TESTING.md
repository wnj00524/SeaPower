# Sea Power acceptance checks

Status: **not run in game**. Static success does not establish campaign playability.

- Enable the mod alongside Broken Trident; verify both campaigns appear independently.
- Start each difficulty and confirm starting points, roster prices, allowed variants and only mission 1 unlocked.
- Buy two or more ships, including sonar/helo ASW and an AAW escort. Inspect generated task-force spacing and anchor replacement. Confirm no placeholder Spruance is duplicated or supplied free.
- Open every mission in the editor. Inspect water depth and all authored tracks, including neutral ships; inspect purchased fleet formations separately. Natural Earth screening is not a depth check.
- Confirm unit types, variants and aircraft loadouts load without substitutions or errors. Verify the name overrides distinguish coalition operators despite stock flags and hulls.
- Fly both ASW scenarios to completion; confirm the submerged patrol is detectable and killable with the available force.
- Observe Skyhawk/F-5 bombing behaviour and MiG-21 interception. Verify enemy AI attacks and that aircraft fuel/airfield limitations do not cause crashes or broken objectives.
- In missions 1 and 5, test protected-ship arrival, protected-ship sinking, timeout and loss of every player ship. Verify the neutral transport actually follows its authored route.
- In mission 4, test survival at 60 minutes and defeat when all player ships sink before then. There is no compulsory enemy kill count.
- In missions 3 and 6, confirm victory requires both named military targets; destroying unrelated traffic must not award victory. In mission 8, confirm all three enemy warships are required.
- Resolve an objective close to a failure timer. Confirm the result does not change during the ten-second exit delay.
- After each victory, inspect fleet damage, ammunition, points and the next mission unlock. Verify maintenance is enabled only at missions 1, 3, 5 and 7 and final mission completion ends the campaign.
- Complete a full Supported playthrough before balancing Standard/Severe. Record ship losses, SAM expenditure, completion time and any AI route abandonment per mission.
